You: Motor Vehicle Advisor: If your car shudders or vibrates when braking at high speed, the most common cause is warped brake rotors (also called brake discs). When the rotors are uneven or have developed high spots due to heat or wear, the brake pads can't grip smoothly, causing a pulsation or shudder through the brake pedal and sometimes the steering wheel.

Other possible causes include:

- **Worn or uneven brake pads:** If the pads are worn unevenly or contaminated, they can cause vibration.
- **Loose or worn suspension components:** Worn ball joints, tie rods, or wheel bearings can amplify vibration during braking.
- **Wheel imbalance or alignment issues:** While these usually cause vibration at speed, they can worsen during braking.
- **Brake caliper problems:** A sticking caliper can cause uneven pad contact.

It's important to have this checked promptly by a qualified mechanic because braking performance is critical for safety. They will inspect the brake rotors, pads, and suspension components and recommend resurfacing or replacing parts as needed.
